Schedule a drop

Members can shop from the first time, everyone else from the second. Products set to “In this drop” follow these times. The countdown on the site updates on its own.

Send early-access links

Every member with an email gets their own private link. Put {link} where it goes. Only people who joined before members’ shopping opens get in. Texts: export the CSV in Members, it has each person’s link.

Add a product

Photos. The first is the main photo; the second shows when shoppers hover in the shop. Square photos on a plain black background look best.
Quick sizes

New products start hidden, so the photos can be edited before anyone sees them. When it’s ready, uncheck “Hide from the site”, then set the status and stock in the list below.

Products & stock

Status: Sold out shows “Notify me”. In this drop follows the drop times. On sale is always buyable. Stock goes down by itself with every order, and a size at 0 shows as sold out unless pre-order is on.
