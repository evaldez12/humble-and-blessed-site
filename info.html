Shipping

Orders ship from New York in two to four business days. You get a tracking number as soon as the label prints.

  • US orders ship for a flat $8, free over $100.
  • Pre-order pieces ship in the window shown on the product page.

Returns and exchanges

Unworn, unwashed pieces with tags can be returned or exchanged for a different size within 14 days of delivery, while stock lasts. Final drops are not restocked.

To start one, email us with your order number and what you need. If something arrives wrong or damaged, send a photo within 7 days and we make it right.

Size guide

Everything runs true to size with a relaxed cut. Between sizes, take the smaller one.

Not sure? Email us your height and usual size and we will tell you which one to get.

Contact

Email hello@humblenblessed.com, or message @humblenblessed on Instagram.

Stockists, collabs and press: same email, with "Wholesale" or "Collab" in the subject.

Privacy

We collect what we need to ship your order and, if you join the list, to email or text you about drops and restocks. Payments are handled by Stripe, so we never see your card number.

We do not use advertising cookies right now. If we add them, you will be asked first and they only load if you say yes. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link.

Terms

All designs, logos and photos belong to Humble & Blessed. Prices are in US dollars. We can cancel and refund an order placed because of a pricing or stock error. A limited drop may post its own rules with the release.
