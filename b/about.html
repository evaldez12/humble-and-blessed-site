Always Be Humble To Stay Blessed.

Humble & Blessed started in 2020 in Rob's apartment in New York with one screen, one blank tee and a praying cherub he had been drawing since high school. The first run was twenty-four shirts. They sold to friends, then to friends of friends, then to people nobody knew.

The cherub is still on everything we make. It is not a logo so much as a reminder: the blessings keep coming as long as you stay humble about them.

We make small runs on heavy fabric and we do not reprint. Every piece is screen-printed or embroidered in New York, and every drop has a number. When a drop is gone it stays gone, which is why the people who wear it tend to know each other.

If you are reading this, you are early. Welcome.
